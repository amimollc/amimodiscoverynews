# Amimo Discovery

**Amimo Discovery** is a news discovery website that brings stories from
independent and established publishers together in one place. Browse
topics, find stories, and follow links back to the publishers that
reported them.

**Website:** https://amimodiscovery.pages.dev\
**Live TV:** https://amimodiscovery.pages.dev/live

## What you can do

-   **Explore news by topic:** Browse categories including For you,
    Interests, Local, World, Politics, Tech, Business, Sports,
    Entertainment, Health, Science, Weather, Education, Gaming, Food,
    Travel, and Automotive.
-   **Search:** Use the search field to look for headlines, topics, and
    sources.
-   **Save stories:** Bookmark articles to revisit later. Saved stories
    may be available offline when they have been cached on your device.
-   **Use an account:** When signed in, saved stories and account
    features can sync across devices. The site also offers options to
    like articles and follow topics.
-   **Watch live channels:** Visit the Live TV page to browse channels
    and search by channel name, country, or language. Availability
    depends on the broadcaster and stream; some streams may be offline
    or restricted.

## AI and automated features

Amimo Discovery is designed to make it easier to discover and understand
current affairs. Where AI-assisted tools or explanations are provided in
the site, treat them as helpful aids---not as the original reporting.

AI-generated summaries or explanations can omit context or make
mistakes. For important matters, open the linked article, check the
publisher and publication date, and compare reliable sources. AI output
is not a substitute for professional advice or an authoritative source.

**Transparency:** The website's main news experience is an aggregation
and discovery feed with publisher links and credits. This README does
not claim that an AI assistant or AI-generated summary is available on
every story or on every visit; use only AI features that are visibly
offered in the app.

## Sources and attribution

Stories are presented with links and credits to their original
publishers where available. Amimo Discovery helps users find coverage;
it does not replace the original publisher. Follow the source link to
read the full report and its context.

## Live TV notes

The Live TV page loads channel information and streams dynamically. A
channel listing does not guarantee that its stream will play in every
country, on every device, or at all times. Playback can be affected by
broadcaster restrictions, connectivity, and stream availability. If a
stream fails, try another channel or return later.

## Privacy and account use

If you choose to sign in, use the account controls provided on the
website. Avoid sharing passwords or sensitive personal information in
public. Review the site's privacy information, if presented, before
deciding what account details to provide.

## Tips for using the site

1.  Choose a topic or search for a headline, subject, or publisher.
2.  Open a story and use its publisher link to read the original report.
3.  Save useful stories for later.
4.  Visit **Live TV** to browse available channels, keeping in mind that
    streams can change or be unavailable.

## Feedback and support

For questions, corrections, or feedback, use the contact or feedback
option provided on the website, if available.
