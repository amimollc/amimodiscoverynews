A guide to Amimo News

One feed. Every newsroom that matters.

Amimo News pulls headlines from dozens of independent publishers into a single, clean feed — personalized to you, local to wherever you are, and yours to read anywhere. Here's everything it can do for you.

Visit now See what's inside

Amimo News is a free news reader — no single publisher, no single point of view. It gathers stories from world, national, and local outlets and sorts them into a feed built around your interests, your language, and your region.

You can read it right in your browser, or install it to your phone or desktop like any other app — including offline, once a page has loaded.

What's inside

The features that make up your everyday reading experience.

A feed built For You

Amimo mixes stories from World, Politics, Technology, Business, Sports, Entertainment and Health into one feed, weighted toward what you actually read.

Local news, automatically

Amimo detects your country on your first visit and surfaces local outlets alongside the national picture — covering well over 60 countries.

Every major topic, one tap away

Jump straight into World, Politics, Technology, Business, Sports, Entertainment, or Health whenever you want to go deep on one subject.

Trending, from everywhere at once

A fast-moving rail of the day's biggest stories, mixed across categories and outlets so no single newsroom dominates it.

Search that actually finds it

One search box checks multiple sources at once, so a niche story is far more likely to turn up than it would on a single outlet's site.

Save it for later

Bookmark any story with one tap and come back to it from any device once you're signed in.

Like stories, follow topics

Liking articles and following a topic both quietly sharpen your For You feed over time — no settings screen required.

One free account, everywhere

Sign in with email or Google and your saved articles, likes and followed topics travel with you to your next device.

Live channels

Browse live news channels alongside your reading feed for when you want to watch as well as read.

Install it like an app

Add Amimo to your home screen for a full-screen experience that opens instantly and keeps working when your connection drops.

A nudge when it matters

Turn on notifications and Amimo will let you know when a major story breaks, without burying you in alerts.

Read it your way

Switch between light and dark themes depending on the time of day or your own preference.

Getting started

From first visit to a feed that actually knows what you like — in a few minutes.

1

Open Amimo News

Visit amimodiscovery.pages.dev in any browser — no download required to start reading.

2

Browse For You, or pick a topic

Start on the personalized feed, or head straight into a category if you already know what you're after.

3

Create a free account

Sign up with an email, or continue with Google in one tap — this is what lets your saved articles and preferences follow you to other devices.

4

Save, like, and follow topics

Every article you save or like — and every topic you follow — quietly teaches your feed what to show you more of.

5

Install it to your home screen

Your browser will offer to install Amimo as an app. Do it for instant loading and offline reading.

6

Turn on notifications

Say yes when prompted, and Amimo will let you know when something big happens.
